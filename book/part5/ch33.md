# Chapter 33 — Communication & Collaboration: The Humans, and the Hybrid

The classic chapter taught the discipline's most-tested tool: **the production meeting**, and the "red team/blue team"-style communication habits that keep reliability a *team* sport (running a schedule, a handover, a rotating chair, standing items, going in with the receipts). All of it survives; the 2026 twist is that *one of your team's best writers, note-takers, and listeners may be a machine.*

## The production meeting (the classic gift, forever)

- **Format:** recurring (weekly), fixed agenda, recycled standing items: open action items → SLO/error-budget dashboard → launch/capacity/major-change reviews → new alerts → toil ledger → AI/agent governance bytes (the 2026 add).
- **Rule of attendance:** *every* team (product, dev, SRE, and — where they exist — ML/AI-platform owners) has *at least one* owner present. The meeting that matters is the one that makes decisions the whole org absorbs, on a schedule.
- **The note pattern:** decisions, owners, and *dates* — not vibes. The AI scribe drafts a suggested minutes; a human spot-checks, corrects, owns.

## Now the 2026 addition: three audiences, not two

**Audience 1 — The humans (unchanged habits, higher stakes):**
Context over completeness; name the risk and the offer to help; ask "what could go wrong?" in both directions; the blameless replay of Chapter 28 as org culture.

**Audience 2 — The machine(s) around you (you must *speak their language* and *require theirs*):**
- Document for *agents*: runbooks ("do this if X"), self-describing playbooks, structured formats the triager can parse (Chapters 22–24, 29).
- The "speak to the intern" habit: clear one-line summarizations, pointers to the evidence (Chapters 25, 27), scoped approvals.
- *Emit telemetry about the brain itself* (Chapters 23–24) — so the machines stay legible to *you*.

**Audience 3 — Machine ↔ machine (the new choreography):**
Agents negotiate, hand off, defer. Require: call-scoped credentials, audit trails, structured handoff docs, and — at the seam before any consequential act — *a human's approval gate* (Chapters 8, 24, 27).

## The "production meeting, 2026 rubric"

| Item (standing) | What the human asks | What the agent adds |
|---|---|---|
| Dashboard / SLO burn | "Are we spending error budget as planned?" (Ch. 5) | Drafted summary + anomaly flags |
| Launch/capacity review | "Is the new thing safe to ramp?" (Ch. 21) | Drafted launch checklist + risk table |
| On-call health | "Are the 3 a.m. pages still as rare?" (Ch. 31) | Toil/interrupt ledger trends |
| AI governance | "Did the agents behave this week?" (Ch. 20) | Judgment-SLI report (Ch. 24) |
| Action items | "Who said they'd do what, by when?" | Auto-tracked + nudged (human approves) |
