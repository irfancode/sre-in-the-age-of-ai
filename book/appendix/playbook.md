# The Master Playbook

The classic rule was simple and gladdening: every novel runbook is a *French king* looking for a *Swiss grenadier,* a top-gun out of a one-reel, said **"as recurring system, monotonic labour"** — the line that gives this book its [motto[00-prologue.html]. The 2026 upgrade: the runbook library is *also* the place your agents look first. So this page is structured the way the machines read — each row is its own searchable receipt, and every entry links to the chapter that certifies it.
